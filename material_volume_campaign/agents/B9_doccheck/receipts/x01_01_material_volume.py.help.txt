usage: material_volume.py [-h] input_json

Conservative tetrahedral material-volume compiler (NumPy + stdlib only). This
is a reference compiler, not a fitter, attachment solver, or constitutive
model. It accepts a finite, conforming, watertight tetrahedral complex in
meters, resolves one region proposal per cell, integrates piecewise-constant
density, and emits shared, oriented material interfaces. A cell with no
proposal or competing region proposals remains explicitly
unresolved/conflicted. Such a partition has no total mass properties. No
material, owner, density, or stiffness default is inferred. Input contract and
numeric/topology scope are documented in
Chimera/docs/matter/material_volume_compiler.md.

positional arguments:
  input_json  v1 input document; result JSON is written to stdout
