**Seed = 0.** The origin. The one number the whole world is derived from.
This folder is the outermost membrane: the complete story. Every membrane below
is nested inside it, and the *path* to any of them is that membrane's whole story.

## The one law of the beginning
- **÷0 is FORBIDDEN** — the singularity, the undefined, the far side of the event
  horizon. Nothing may divide by the seed.
- **+matter is ALLOWED** — the only legal first act. Add a cloud of matter to the
  void; the center of its mass is where gravity will come from.

So gravity is **derived, not assumed** — it is the *output* of "what we added to 0."

## Determinism
Same seed → same universe, forever. The world is a pure function of 0 and the laws
nested below it. (CLAUDE.md: "same seed, same world, forever.")

## The scene is scoped by the goal
An author can describe the universe in one sentence or twenty trillion. **The goal
narrows it:** the game is a player exploring down to where a foot meets the ground,
so this story renders a *solar system → planet → ground → body → walking → contact*
and leaves the far universe as a one-sentence backdrop. Detail is spent where the
player goes.

## Why (terminal → PHYSICS)
This membrane asserts only arithmetic: 0 is the additive identity; division by 0 is
undefined. True in an empty universe. No observer needed.
