# theHorizon

*Paragraph 2.*

Since division by zero is forbidden, the only legal act is addition — and the instant you add mass
to the point, the math hands back something that is **not** zero: a length. `r_s = 2GM/c²`. Mass at
a point creates a horizon, and a horizon is a boundary, and a boundary is a scale — so **the first
membrane in the universe is born from the first addition, not declared.**

The same addition read from the other end gives the quantum's length, `λ_C = ħ/mc`; one grows with
mass, the other shrinks, so there is exactly one mass where they are equal —
`2GM/c² = ħ/Mc  →  M = √(ħc/2G) ≈ 10⁻⁸ kg` — and at that crossing the black hole and the electron
are the same **size** as well as the same three numbers.

That crossing sets the universe's first units: `l_P = √(ħG/c³) = 1.616×10⁻³⁵ m` and
`t_P = l_P/c = 5.39×10⁻⁴⁴ s`. Below that length the horizon would have to fit inside its own
quantum and the forbidden division returns — so **zero is not approachable, it is fenced**, and the
fence is the smallest legal membrane. Every membrane after this is that same fence redrawn at a
larger radius.
