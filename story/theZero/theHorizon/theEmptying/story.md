*Paragraph 3.*

The fence is not a line, it is a **surface**, and a surface has area — `A = 4πr_s²` — which the only
available unit must measure: `S = kA/4l_P²`, one quarter-bit per Planck square. That is the whole

quantum emitted makes it hotter and faster (`lifetime ∝ M³`), so the giving-back is not a leak but a
runaway that ends all at once.

across exactly the extent the horizon defined, one Planck cell at a time. Paragraph 1 was the point;
