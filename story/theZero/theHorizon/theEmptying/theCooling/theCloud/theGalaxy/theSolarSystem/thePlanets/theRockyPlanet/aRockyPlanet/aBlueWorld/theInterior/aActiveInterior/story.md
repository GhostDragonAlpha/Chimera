**In plain words —** The inside this planet actually has: a working engine. 23 terawatts leaving
through rock that creeps two centimetres a year, an iron heart a third of the way to the centre
freezing slowly and powering a magnetic field that holds the star's wind five and a half planet-
radii away.

*An instance of `theInterior`, named by its thermal state: active, Earth's own class.*

## The heat split

Of the 23.0 TW the planet's own solution says it loses: **12.5 TW is radiogenic** (chondritic
production over the silicate mass, measured), **1.7 TW is latent** (the inner core freezing,
scaled from Earth by core mass), and **8.9 TW is primordial** — the planet still paying down the
heat of its own assembly. The gradient at the surface is **22 K per kilometre**.

## The stirring

The Rayleigh number clears the 1,100 threshold by four orders of magnitude — the mantle
convects, at **1.5 cm a year**, and the plates ride it at the same pace. Spreading ridges open
at twice that.

## The field

The dynamo's dipole comes out at **1.6×10²² A·m²**, a surface field of **22 µT** at the pole —
a third of Earth's, but enough: against this star's wind at 1.04 AU, the pressure balance stands
the magnetopause at **6.9 planet radii**, well past the atmosphere it protects.

## The exhale and the ores

Volcanism returns **1.3×10¹¹ kg of CO₂ a year** (Earth's 2.6×10¹¹ scaled by the heat share) —
the carbon cycle's return path, and ultimately the source of the air theAtmosphere keeps. The
crust carries measured abundances: **5.6% iron, 60 ppm copper**; nine-tenths of the siderophiles
sank to the core, where the real metal is.

## The honest anchors, said plainly

The shell thicknesses (30 km continental, 7 km oceanic), the core temperatures (scaled from
Earth's melting curve by central pressure), the wave speeds, and the hotspot count are Earth
anchors or scalings — named as such, none fitted. The planet's own derived numbers (core radius,
heat budget, dynamo verdict, central pressure) arrive through the parent and are never computed
twice.

## The free dials

None. The planet decided all of it above.
