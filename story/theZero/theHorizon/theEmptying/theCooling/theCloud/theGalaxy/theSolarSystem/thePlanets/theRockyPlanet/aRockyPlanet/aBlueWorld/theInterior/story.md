**In plain words —** A rocky planet is shells: the heavy sank and the light floated, so it has an
iron heart wrapped in rock wrapped in a thin skin. Everything else — the heat, the stirring, the
magnetic field, the volcanoes — is that one fact's engine running.

*The law of planetary insides. The instance of it here is `aActiveInterior`.*

## Heat: it is all the same heat leaving

Accretion left the inside hot; radioactivity (uranium, thorium, potassium — chondritic, ~5
picowatts per kilogram, measured) keeps feeding it. The heat leaves through the top, and on its
way out it is **everything**: the geothermal gradient, the convection, the volcanism, and the
magnetic field are the same joule four times.

## Stirring: heated from below, it moves

A fluid heated from below convects when its Rayleigh number clears ~1,100 (measured threshold).
The mantle creeps at centimetres a year and drags the crust with it — plates. No convection, no
plates, no carbon cycle, no continents worth the name.

## The dynamo: a spinning sphere of liquid metal is a magnet

A liquid outer core, convecting, on a spinning planet, is a dynamo — and the field it makes
stands the star's wind off the atmosphere. When the heat runs out, the core freezes through, the
dynamo dies, and the air follows. That is why the classification of an interior is its **thermal
state**: active (Earth), stagnant (Mars), dead (the Moon). An instance is named by its class,
computed from its own heat, dynamo and convection — never assigned.

## The return path

Volcanism outgasses: the atmosphere above is, ultimately, the interior exhaling. And the inner
core freezing releases latent heat — which is what keeps the dynamo alive for billions of years.
