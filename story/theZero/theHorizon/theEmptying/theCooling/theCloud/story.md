**In plain words —** While light was still shoving matter around, nothing could clump. The moment matter turned neutral, light let go — and gravity finally had something to pull on.

*Chapter 5.*

Gravity has no threshold, but it does have an opponent: **pressure**, which pushes back the instant
anything is squeezed. Which one wins is not a matter of degree — Jeans settles it exactly, and the
number that decides is the **sound speed**, which had just collapsed.

While matter was ionized it was welded to the photons, so the gas carried radiation's own stiffness
and its sound speed was relativistic, `c/√3`. The smallest mass gravity was permitted to hold was
then ~10¹⁷ M☉ — larger than anything that existed — so nothing could fall together, and the
unevenness could only **ring**, as sound.

The instant atoms formed and the photons went free, the gas was left with nothing but its own
thermal speed:

```
c_s = √(γkT/μm_H) = 6.5 km/s          (27,000× slower than before)
M_J ∝ c_s³                            → thirteen orders of magnitude smaller
M_J ≈ 6×10⁵ M☉
```

So **transparency was never only the release of light — it was the removal of what had been holding
matter apart.** The one part in 100,000 that had been ringing now had nothing pushing back, and
every region above `M_J` stopped ringing and started falling.

The sea breaks into clouds: about a million solar masses each, neutral hydrogen and helium, getting
denser and hotter as they fall. What is handed on is a bound, collapsing cloud — and a cloud that
cannot stop falling has only one place to go.

## What it predicted that it was never given

`M_jeans_solar` = **607,000 solar masses** — the smallest cloud that can collapse under its own
gravity, once the gas has gone neutral and its sound speed has collapsed with it.

    literature: the Jeans mass at recombination is ~10^5 - 10^6 solar masses

That is the **globular cluster** mass scale, and it is one of the oldest results in structure
formation. Nothing here was aimed at it: the inputs are the parent's recombination temperature and
the density at that epoch, and the sound speed falls by a factor of **26,700** the moment the
electrons are captured — because a neutral gas cannot hold pressure the way a plasma can. The mass
that gravity can beat jumps by the cube of that, and it lands on the size of the first bound
objects anyone has ever counted.
