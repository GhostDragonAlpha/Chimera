# theZero

*Paragraph 1.*

The story starts as a black hole, and a black hole is defined by one number: **zero**. The
singularity is `r = 0` — that is the whole definition, and every "infinity" ever quoted about a
black hole is the same single act: dividing by it. There is exactly one forbidden operation in the
entire object and it happens once, at zero.

It is also an electron. A black hole is completely specified by three numbers — mass, charge,
angular momentum — and an electron has exactly those three and nothing else. Carter showed in 1968
that the Kerr–Newman metric carrying the electron's own numbers returns a gyromagnetic ratio of
exactly `g = 2`, the Dirac electron's value. Two objects at opposite ends of the scale ladder,
described by the same three parameters, giving the same answer. **The ladder is one structure, not
a stack of unrelated scales** — and this is where it is fastened.

So the seed is zero: you cannot divide by it, you can only **add** to it. Everything that follows
— every membrane, out to a footprint on a planet — is what got added.
