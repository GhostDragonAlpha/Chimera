# theZero — the first law: one point, the draw, the resistance

Out of nothing, a point. It has mass and charge — no authored properties — and it
feels two things: the DRAW (every point pulls every point) and the RESISTANCE (near
the contact scale, the draw turns away). There is no other input. Everything in this
story is that law, folded into more points.

**In plain words —** The origin. One law — a point under draw and resistance —
and every other membrane is that law with more points, arranged.

**STATEMENT:** A single point under the draw and the resistance is the only law;
every membrane in this tree is the same point repeated and folded, so no membrane
adds a new force — only a new arrangement of the points that obey the one law.

**PREDICTION:** The light era's record (theLight) will fall, touch, and pack under
exactly this law — the walls and bonds it shows will be the resistance awakening in
the leaves where points touch, not material properties someone added.

**FALSIFIER:** If any membrane in the tree needs a force the origin does not have,
the law was not the law — it was a guess, and the derivation from here is broken.
