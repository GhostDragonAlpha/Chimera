## Geology (20 items)

### Igneous Rocks

#### Basalt (Basalt_Data, Basalt_Specimen)
- **Source:** USGS "Basalt" — United States Geological Survey volcanic rock fact sheet. Available at: https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/basalt
- **Source:** Marshak, S. (2019). *Earth: Portrait of a Planet* (6th ed.). W.W. Norton & Company. Chapter 6: "Igneous Rocks." ISBN: 978-0393640179
- **VERIFY:** Specific claim about olivine and pyroxene in Titan's cryovolcanic plains — general mineralogy is correct (olivine + pyroxene are typical basalt constituents), but Titan-specific basalt composition is inferred from Cassini VIMS data.

#### Granite (Granite_Data)
- **Source:** USGS "Granite" — United States Geological Survey. Available at: https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/granite
- **Source:** Blatt, H., Tracy, R.J., & Owens, B.E. (2006). *Petrology: Igneous, Sedimentary, and Metamorphic* (3rd ed.). W.H. Freeman. Chapter 4: "Granitic Rocks." ISBN: 978-0716737438
- **Note:** Feldspar, quartz, and mica as granite constituents is standard petrology, confirmed by Blatt et al. §4.2.

#### Obsidian (Obsidian_Data)
- **Source:** USGS "Obsidian" — United States Geological Survey. Available at: https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/obsidian
- **Source:** Nave, R. (2023). "Obsidian Formation." *HyperPhysics*, Georgia State University. Available at: http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/Geophys/obsidian.html
- **Note:** Rapid cooling preventing crystal formation is textbook igneous petrology (Marshak 2019, §6.3).

#### Pumice (Pumice_Data)
- **Source:** USGS "Pumice" — United States Geological Survey. Available at: https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/pumice
- **Source:** Whitham, A.G. & Sparks, R.S.J. (1986). "Pumice." *Bulletin of Volcanology*, 48(4), 209-226. DOI: 10.1007/BF01087675
- **Note:** Floating pumice rafts as biological dispersal vectors is documented in Bryan et al. (2012), *Nature Geoscience*.

### Sedimentary Rocks

#### Sandstone (Sandstone_Data)
- **Source:** USGS "Sandstone" — United States Geological Survey. Available at: https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/sandstone

- **VERIFY:** Titan methane dunes — Cassini RADAR data (Radebaugh et al. 2010, *Icarus*) confirms methane dunes on Titan, but the comparison to terrestrial sandstone cementation is analogical.

#### Shale (Shale_Data)

#### Cumulus (Cumulus_Data)
- **Source:** NOAA National Weather Service. "Cumulus." Available at: https://www.weather.gov/jetstream/cumulus
- **Source:** WMO (2017). *International Cloud Atlas*. "Cumulus."
- **Note:** Lifting condensation level (flat cloud base) is thermodynamic standard, detailed in Stull (2017) *Practical Meteorology* §5.3.

#### Stratus (Stratus_Data)
- **Source:** NOAA National Weather Service. "Stratus." Available at: https://www.weather.gov/jetstream/stratus
- **Source:** WMO (2017). *International Cloud Atlas*. "Stratus."
- **VERIFY:** Titan methane stratus — Cassini data confirms methane stratus clouds (Griffith et al. 2005, *Science*), but Titan comparison is our own educational framing.

#### Storm Systems (Storm_Data)
- **Source:** NOAA National Weather Service. "Thunderstorm Life Cycle." Available at: https://www.weather.gov/jetstream/tstorm

- **VERIFY:** Titan methane storms lasting weeks — Cassini observed extended storm periods (Turtle et al. 2011, *Science*).

#### Lightning (Lightning_Data)

- **Source:** Waite, J.H. et al. (2007). "The Process of Tholin Formation in Titan's Upper Atmosphere." *Science*, 316(5826), 870-875. DOI: 10.1126/science.1139727
