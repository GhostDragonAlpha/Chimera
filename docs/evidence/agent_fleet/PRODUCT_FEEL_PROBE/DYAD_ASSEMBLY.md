**PRODUCT-FEEL VERDICT: HUMAN-LEGIBLE (rubric PASS, 2/2), with five named
defects between this and a product — the judge's own question-4 critique,
recorded as the gap list, not paraphrased:**

1. Only the right arm visibly moves; the "28 joints" story is carried by UI
   text and reel thumbnails, not observable motion.
2. The SHOW banner reads "theta 0.00 deg" while naming a joint OTHER than the
   one moving (banner cycles independent of motion) — a naive viewer cannot
   tell which number belongs to which joint, and the zero contradicts the
   nonzero green labels.
3. The head is cropped by the viewport in every frame — the face joints the
   reel claims to exercise cannot be judged.
4. TIMING UNTRUTH: the frames span a declared 4.5 s but the on-screen show
   clock advances ~36 s per frame and wraps a 112 s lap — the product surface
   misstates time to any viewer who reads it.
5. gait steps=0, feet never move — no locomotion; "alive" is not demonstrated.

**Reading for the roadmap**: the physics is already legible — a stranger
named the behavior and stated the lesson unprompted. What stands between this
and a product is presentation-truth: motion that shows what the text claims,
UI numbers coupled to the joints they describe, framing that fits the subject,
a clock that tells the truth, and eventually locomotion. These five are
natural product-surface work items; defects 2 and 4 are product-truth defects
(numbers lying on screen), the class this project refuses everywhere else.
