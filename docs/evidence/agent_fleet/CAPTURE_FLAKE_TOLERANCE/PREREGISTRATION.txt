## THE MECHANISM (theory of the flake, stated before the run)

The fixture window class is registered with CS_HREDRAW|CS_VREDRAW and a
GRAY_BRUSH background. In step 2 of the flaky test, resize_client() moves
the real window (SetWindowPos), which queues erase/paint work; the test
then re-measures, repaint()s the deterministic pattern (a direct DC blit),
and immediately calls verify_hwnd_capture -> PrintWindow. Under suite load
the queued erase can land AFTER the blit (PrintWindow and GetClientRect
can dispatch queued sent messages), so the captured surface is a half-
erased (gray) or mid-state window and the content gate fails closed as
occluded_or_foreign_content. Same fixture, same deterministic pattern, a
moment later: the capture is exact. That is a transient window-state race,
not a contract defect -- and the gate behaved CORRECTLY when it fired
(fail-closed on content deviation is the contract working).

## STATEMENT

The flake is a transient-window-state race, not a contract defect; a
bounded, evidence-retaining retry (with the failure retained on first
attempt) makes the test deterministic WITHOUT weakening what it asserts.

## FALSIFIERS (what would kill this deliverable)

F-A: the retry masks a REAL occlusion -- i.e. the tolerance is not
     time-bounded, or retries more than once, or retries against drifted
     fixture parameters, or a persistent occlusion ends in anything other
     than the original assertion failing with both records retained.
F-B: any assertion removed or weakened anywhere in the module (checked by
     diff: the assertion set is unchanged; retry wraps, never skips).
F-C: failures not retained -- a healed transient whose first-attempt
     record is discarded rather than carried on the returned record
     (and surfaced in the evidence *.txt when one occurs naturally).
D:   suite failures anywhere (the fleet suite must stay green).

## IMPLEMENTATION SHAPE (bound on the run)

expected_pattern, pinned_size, title, ...) in test_capture_window.py:
