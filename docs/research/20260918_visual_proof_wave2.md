4. A graph rebuild is non-idempotent, sharded records are omitted/duplicated, Graphify changes authored content, or evidence count is not exactly 11.
5. Any renderer silently fills a missing value, invents a spatial interpretation, loses sentinel markers, mislabels WorldCover permanent water, omits Macaca specimen 127, or fails to show all 21 Macaca binomials beneath genus taxon 9539.
