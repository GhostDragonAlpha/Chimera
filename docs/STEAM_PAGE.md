# Deep Space Trader: Educational Frontier

## Tagline
**The solar system is your classroom.** Walk on Titan. Read the rocks. Predict the weather. Navigate by the stars. Every fact is real science — verified, interactive, and built into living game systems.

## Short Description (Steam)
Deep Space Trader: Educational Frontier is an open-world educational RPG set on Saturn's moon Titan — built with Unreal Engine 5.8 and verified against real geology, meteorology, and astronomy. No fictional lore. No made-up science. Just the universe as it actually works, turned into a game you explore on foot.

Identify igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rock by their visible grain structure. Read methane clouds to predict Titan's weather. Watch Saturn hang motionless in the sky — exactly as tidal locking dictates. Collect specimens, trade at orbital stations, manage your suit's oxygen reserves, and survive an alien world by learning how it actually works.

**38 educational discoveries. 41 collectible science specimens. 27 interconnected game systems.** All running in a 455MB standalone build that boots in seconds.

## About This Demo
Explore a 4km² Titan canyon rendered with orange tholin haze, a methane lake, and a cryovolcano cone — all geologically accurate. The demo includes:

- **38 educational text markers** covering real geology (igneous/sedimentary/metamorphic rock identification), meteorology (cloud classification, storm formation, Titan's methane cycle), and astronomy (tidal locking, orbital mechanics, Saturn's rings, stellar navigation)
- **41 collectible specimen items** across three scientific categories — MineralSpecimen, AtmosphericData, AstronomicalData — each with real science descriptions
- **Full survival layer**: oxygen management, battery drain, dust clog, shelter refill — your suit's life-support systems teach real EVA engineering
- **AI-optimized economy**: the game's trade pricing was trained through evolutionary optimization (score: 0.9481/1.0) — prices respond to supply, demand, and route decay, creating a living market that rewards exploration
- **Titan-accurate environment**: orange tholin atmospheric haze (RGB 0.9/0.5/0.2), liquid methane lake, cryovolcano cone, and sunset lighting at 17.5h Titan local time

## Features

### 🔬 Real Geology — 20 Topics
Identify rocks by visible characteristics in canyon walls. Basalt's fine grain means rapid cooling. Granite's large crystals mean slow underground solidification. Sandstone's cross-bedding reveals ancient wind direction. Each formation tells a real geological story. Topics: Basalt, Granite, Obsidian, Pumice, Sandstone, Limestone, Shale, Marble, Quartzite, Slate, and three collections (Igneous, Sedimentary, Metamorphic) plus Titan-specific cryovolcanism.

### 🌤️ Real Meteorology — 13 Topics
Titan has a methane weather cycle eerily similar to Earth's water cycle. Read the clouds: cirrus means high-altitude ice, cumulus means rising warm air, stratus means stable blanket. Track storms, lightning, and wind patterns. Understand how atmospheric pressure drives weather across the solar system. Topics: Cirrus, Cumulus, Stratus, Storm, Lightning, Wind, Clear Sky, Calm, Methane Lakes, Titan Atmosphere.

### 🌌 Real Astronomy — 16 Topics
Saturn's rings, tidal locking, light-travel time, planetary rotation, stellar fusion, exoplanets, dark matter, the cosmic microwave background. Every text is verifiable against real astronomical data. Navigate by the stars — Saturn hangs motionless in Titan's sky, exactly as tidal locking predicts. Topics: Stars, Constellations, Celestial Navigation, Planet Rotation, Gravity, Light Travel, Planet Formation, Moons, Saturn, Solar System, Milky Way, Exoplanets, Deep Space, Titan, Saturn's Rings, Tidal Locking.

### 🚀 27 Interconnected Game Systems
The educational content is not wall text — it's built into living game systems:

- **Suit Life Support** (O₂, battery, dust, temperature) — learn real EVA engineering
- **Economy System** (AI-trained pricing, supply/demand, route decay) — learn market dynamics
- **Inventory & Trading** (pickup, drop, buy, sell across 3 stations) — learn resource management
- **Faction System** (reputation with station factions) — learn social dynamics
- **Mission System** (accept, complete, fail — procedural objectives) — learn goal management
- **Survival Shelter** (O₂ regeneration, dust scrubbing) — learn habitat engineering
- **Player Controller** (third-person camera, spring-arm, gesture wheel, jump, crouch, sprint)
- **PCG Procedural Terrain** (landscape generation, clutter scattering, biome zoning)
- **Day/Night Cycle** (real-time sun position, volumetric clouds, fog)
- **Character System** (astronaut BP with idle/walk/jump animations)
- **AI NPCs** (traders, pirates with behavior trees)
- **Travel System** (quantum travel specs, trade routes, orbital mechanics)
- **Save/Load System** (persistent progression)
- +14 more subsystems including audio, VFX, combat, flight, ships, materials, movement, tools, sound, and environment

### 🤖 AI-Trained Balance
The economy wasn't hand-tuned by a designer — it was evolved. The game's internal trainer runs thousands of simulations per second, optimizing prices, supply elasticity, and route decay toward a single objective: "Money should come from working the whole map across several commodities, not from one wiki-known loop." The result is a balanced, deep trading experience that rewards exploration and penalizes grinding. Training score: **0.9481 / 1.0**.

### 🎮 Built with Unreal Engine 5.8
Volumetric clouds. Procedural terrain landscapes. Real-time exponential height fog. Dynamic sky spheres with configurable sun position. Shipping build: **455MB** — boots in seconds, runs on mid-range hardware.
