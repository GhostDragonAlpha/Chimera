> Researched 2026-07-28, after the gait witness convicted our walk (**periodicity 0.24**, airborne 15%,
> asymmetric toe-drag). Two of these findings target that failure directly, and a third says our
> *exploration method is the known-worst option* for a 290-muscle body. Ranked by what it changes here.

---

## 1. MUSCLE SYNERGIES — and we are already in position to use them

**The finding.** Muscles do not fire independently; biology coordinates them in **synergies**
(co-contracting groups). **SAR** (Berg et al., RSS 2023) extracts these automatically: train a policy
on a **simpler task**, aggregate its muscle activations, project them with **normalized ICA-PCA** onto
a low-dimensional manifold, then use that manifold as the **action basis** for the harder task.
Reported to enable "robust transfer learning to far more complex tasks."

**Why it matters to us.** Our action space is **290 independent muscles**. SAR would collapse that to
roughly 10–20 synergy dimensions — a ~20× smaller search.

**And the recipe fits what we already built:** SAR needs a policy trained on a *simpler* task to mine
synergies from. **We have exactly that — the trained STAND policy** (`myobody_policy.pt`, 77% survival).
Extract synergies from its activations → use as the action basis for the walk. No new prerequisite.

## 2. CENTRAL PATTERN GENERATORS — rhythm by construction, aimed straight at periodicity 0.24

**The finding.** In mammals the **spinal cord contains a CPG** that produces the basic locomotor rhythm
**without input from the brain or sensory feedback**. **CPG-RL** couples this with RL: instead of
emitting raw muscle activations, the policy **modulates oscillator setpoints (amplitude, frequency,
phase)**. **AI-CPG** does this for *bipedal* locomotion through reinforced reflex networks.

**Why it matters to us.** Our single convicting number was **periodicity 0.24 — "no repeating cycle."**
A CPG makes the cycle **structural**: rhythm is generated by coupled oscillators, so periodicity is
near-1.0 *by construction*, and the policy's job shrinks to *shaping* the rhythm rather than inventing
it from scratch. This is the most direct answer to the witness's verdict that exists.

**It also matches this project's own architecture:** rhythm generated *low* in the spine, shaped by
higher drive, is exactly the reflex/membrane split already used ([[process-not-position]]).

## 3. OUR EXPLORATION IS THE KNOWN-WORST OPTION

**The finding.** For musculoskeletal *walking*, both **SAR** and **Latent Exploration (LATTICE)**
"outperform **unbounded Gaussian** methods asymptotically." Best reported setup: **Latent Exploration
for synergistic exploration of agonistic muscles + an out-of-bounds penalty** → near-optimal, and
aligned with motion-capture data.

**Why it matters to us.** `train_myobody_gait.py` samples **independent Gaussian noise per muscle on
290 muscles** — precisely the "unbounded Gaussian" baseline these papers beat. Independent noise across
290 muscles is mostly *incoherent thrash*; correlated/latent noise explores *coordinated* movements.

## 4. Supporting work worth mining

- **Bioinspired morphology + task curricula** (Nature Comms Eng, 2025): curricula combining **physical
  growth** and skill progression produce gaits adaptable across speeds and terrains. Growing the body as
  part of the curriculum resonates with this project's growth model.
- **FreeMusco** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025): **motion-free** latent control for musculoskeletal characters —
  no motion-capture required (our exact constraint), combining musculoskeletal sim with model-based RL
  for biomechanically plausible, expressive motion.
- **MyoChallenge 2025** (~70 teams, 560+ submissions; models ~3× more complex than prior years): winning
  approaches used **physics-based motion planners, on-policy behaviour cloning, hierarchical planning,
  and muscle synergies**. Synergies appear again, in the winners' toolkit.
- **Adversarial imitation** on a 16-DOF / 92-MTU humanoid produced "natural-looking gaits with only a
  few demonstrations" — the AMP path, once we have any reference motion.

## What this changes here

| our measured failure | the frontier's answer |
|---|---|
| periodicity 0.24 — no cycle | **CPG**: rhythm by construction |
| 290-dim search is enormous | **SAR synergies** from the existing STAND policy (~20× smaller) |
| per-muscle independent noise | **Latent/correlated exploration** + out-of-bounds penalty |
| no mocap available | **FreeMusco** (motion-free) — and AMP later if we source data |

**Recommended order** (cheapest-first, each independently testable):
1. **SAR synergies** — mine the STAND policy we already have; biggest win per unit of work, no new theory.
2. **CPG rhythm layer** — the direct fix for periodicity; compose with synergies (biologically the CPG
   *drives* the synergies, so the pairing is the real spinal architecture).
3. **Latent exploration** — a smaller change to how PPO samples noise.
4. Curricula / growth, FreeMusco-style latent control, AMP when motion data exists.
