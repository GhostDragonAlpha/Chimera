## LANE 2: LOCOMOTION (Policy + Objective)

### What was built

**Objective diagnosis** (`docs/LOCOMOTION_OBJECTIVE_DIAGNOSIS.md`) — the gold-standard document for this lane. Headline finding:

> Near the incumbent, `stand_reward` correlates with survival at **r = −0.162** (CI includes zero). The multiplicative structure inverts because `height` and `joints` are anti-correlated at −0.902, and `joints` predicts survival at **−0.530** (wrong sign) while being the strongest single factor.

### What was measured (ablation, 12×15 budget, warm start = incumbent)

| Policy class | Objective | Held-out survival | Δ from warm start |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| PD | full | 6.84 s | +0.02 s |
| P-only | full | 6.88 s | +0.06 s |
| PD | support_only | 6.84 s | +0.02 s |
| P-only | support_only | 6.88 s | +0.06 s |

**Result: The objective makes no measurable difference.** Support-only performs identically to full on both policy classes. This validates the diagnosis — near the incumbent, the objective's correlation with survival is noise, and removing the broken multiplicative structure gains nothing but clarity.

### Rule established (binding)

> **NO POLICY-CLASS EXPERIMENT MAY BE RANKED BY THIS OBJECTIVE.** Near the incumbent, r = −0.162. Every comparison is judged on HELD-OUT SURVIVAL directly. (`--judge` refuses anything but `held_out`.)
