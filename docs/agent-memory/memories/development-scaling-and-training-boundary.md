**THE OPERATOR'S SCALING QUESTION (2026-09-21):** "I thought there would be a way to train the whole thing all at once given bone dimensions + joint ranges... if we have to go through this entire process for everything, we'll never complete a complete video game. What's the time frame for an entire game with these methods, and which/where enhancements reduce it WITHOUT violating the spirit of the project?"

**WHY NOT END-TO-END TRAINING (the delivered answer, three grounds):** (1) a trained policy is a million free numbers; the project's law is zero free numbers — a network that walks can't say why, failures aren't mineable to named laws, falsifiers can't be pre-registered, byte-exact verification impossible; (2) gradient descent solves sim artifacts not physics — the exact failure class we fight (sub-micron touch grazes deciding calendars, ledger overruns) is what RL policies "solve" by exploiting contact-solver bugs (hopping-by-restitution class); (3) THE LAWS ARE THE PRODUCT — physics-*teaching* game; the explainable body of law is the content players learn from; a walking blob is worthless here.

**THE PROPOSE/CERTIFY BOUNDARY (the spirit-compatible answer, standing doctrine):** training can PROPOSE, derivation can CERTIFY — same instrument law as the dyad's vision model. An RL policy never ships; its successful trajectories are mined as evidence for derived laws. Fast search, slow proof — the same boundary the project has always drawn between machine and verdict. Guardrails: nothing trains-then-ships; no tuning-to-pass; every visual still faces dyad + operator eyes.

**TIMEFRAMES DELIVERED (2026-09-21):** walking monkey days (in flight); physics core (walk + rear-up + grasp + terrain) 2–5 weeks WITH enhancements (~2 months without); early-access game 3–6 months with enhancements (9–12 without). THE KEY ECONOMICS: the FIRST creature is the expensive one — the walk's 37 laws build the touch/balance/exchange/calendar substrate every future motion reuses; THE ALIVENESS LAW makes creature #2 a fraction of creature #1; the constraint ledger turns reuse into literal conjunction.

**RANKED ENHANCEMENTS (all spirit-compatible):** (1) THE TRACE-ORACLE — RL policies trained fast in our sim as disposable evidence-generators; harvest successful strides; mine them for laws; attacks the mining bottleneck (the wave's deepest cost), est. 5–10× candidate supply; the closest thing to "train it all at once" the spirit permits; (2) races-as-default (wave-27 pattern generalized — 5–8 candidate lanes per open problem, best measured wins); (3) the surrogate scorer (GP/Bayesian over the ~100-run verified wave corpus with uncertainty; Rule-0 falsifier = pre-registered predictions for the next wave, hit-rate vs random; trigger = two consecutive waves spending >50% budget on mines a feature-ranking would skip); (4) constraint-ledger migration (laws as records → new motions inherit substrate by conjunction); (5) the k-gate email (unblocks the rear-up/biped class); (6) product breadth stays on parallel lanes (never rides the serial spine). Related: [[parallel-without-worktrees-vision]] (the parallelism half of the same directive), [[astra-consultation-channel]] (the constraint-ledger spec), [[gait-walk-campaign-20260920]] (the serial spine), [[alan-fleet-operating-style]].

**ALAN'S ORIGINAL VISION CONFIRMED REALIZABLE (2026-09-21 follow-up: "I thought we would create a Phys backbone and train everything as a policy, rewards that would guide and shape development — that was kind of my idea"):** his vision decomposes into layers that ALL survive, with one split — **the physics backbone already exists** (matter kernel + engine; every policy/candidate/race runs against the same byte-exact physics), and **rewards that guide and shape development are already in hand** because the project's acceptance bars ARE reward functions waiting to be written down: **reward = ticks survived + letters passed − joules burned − falsifiers fired** (not designed-from-taste — the project's own measured quality criteria, formalized). Train policies against it in-sim (fast, parallel, throwaway) as SEARCH ENGINES that find the behaviors the bars define; then the trace-oracle mines winning trajectories into derived laws. THE ONE SPLIT (standing doctrine): rewards shape the SEARCH; derivation certifies the RESULT — the trained policy never ships. Even the training is Rule-0-able: pre-register "a policy under reward R reaches N ticks; its best trajectory yields a law passing letters L" (a falsifiable claim about the search itself). THE END-STATE STACK, in Alan's terms: physics backbone (built) → reward-shaped search (his idea, as the proposal engine) → record DSL (his idea, as data not syntax) → derived certified laws (the spirit, unchanged) — each layer makes the one above it cheaper.

**THE WARP FP64 WALL (2026-09-21, diagnosed on the full-body GPU port lane at the operator's request — a STANDING TECHNICAL CONSTRAINT for every future GPU-port lane):** Warp on consumer GeForce (RTX 4090) has weak float64 kernel coverage — many math ops have no fp64 overload, surfacing as "Couldn't find function overload for '<kernel>' that matched inputs with types: [...float64...]" — and a monolithic step kernel with 30+ flat fixedarray arguments fights Warp's launch/type-resolution model. THE FIX PATTERN (sent to the lane; generalizes): (1) STATE DEVICE-RESIDENT — the kernel takes an env index + array views and reads/writes state in place (P2's slice_gpu.py is the in-house working example); constants ride a by-value struct; (2) f32 SIM-WIDE — the precision loss is already priced by train-approximate/certify-exact (P2 measured f32 one-step 4.6e-7 quiescent / 2e-5 contact, bounded rollouts); class bars in preregs, quantized matched inits, documented per-step error; NEVER chase fp64 on consumer silicon; (3) split monolithic step kernels (phase kernels) if the signature still fights; (4) timebox framework-fighting ~2h then escalate — fallback Numba-CUDA with device-resident state. NEVER bisect the framework symptom-by-symptom (the lane's _bisect_mod.py grinding was the anti-pattern).

**THE TWO-TYPE DEVELOPMENT LAW (2026-09-21, operator directive: "we need two types of development — that method is correct for making the STRUCTURE itself but for making things MOVE we may need a machine learning approach... larger GPU runs not broken down") — ADOPTED AS PROGRAM LAW.** TYPE A — STRUCTURE (the current method, unchanged forever): anatomy, joints, muscles, the physics kernel, terrain, the environments themselves — derived, cited, byte-exact, Rule-0; nothing here gets trained, ever. TYPE B — MOVEMENT (learned): policies driving the derived body through complex environments (the simulated forest vision), trained as monolithic GPU runs NOT broken down (his instinct is correct: policy training is undecomposable gradient flow — the mirror image of Type A's one-law-per-wave). The biology parallel delivered back to him: bodies are built slow, movement is learned fast — his split matches how animals actually work. THE LAYERING (options framework): the walk campaign's 38 laws ARE balance reflexes (touch-reset, capture, height emergency) — derived reflexes underneath, learned selection on top (the learned layer chooses when to walk/where to step/when to rear; the derived layer keeps it standing while it decides). THE THREE BOUNDARIES KEEPING TYPE B IN-SPIRIT: (1) rewards-from-bars with Astra's split standing (shaping for training; acceptance enforces every hard condition independently — no falls, clears obstacle, CoT in budget — then operator eyes + dyad); (2) TRAIN APPROXIMATE, CERTIFY EXACT — policies train fast in a GPU-tier sim, acceptance runs in the byte-exact CPU engine (the verification farm, 31s certified runs, is the Type-B gate; sim-to-sim transfer is far easier honesty than sim-to-real); (3) the learned policy is a TENANT of the derived world, never a replacement — no reward hacking survives the exact-sim gate. THE GATING PREREQUISITE (first Type-B lane, not yet dispatched): SIMULATION THROUGHPUT — the engine is CPU single-threaded (~10 ticks/s; training needs millions); the lane = the GPU/parallel physics tier (batched, headless, throughput-tuned, certified against the CPU engine at the physics-summary level — one set of physics records compiled to both targets, exactly what the constraint ledger enables; the 4090 mostly idle, the compute harness's measured 13× is the down payment).
