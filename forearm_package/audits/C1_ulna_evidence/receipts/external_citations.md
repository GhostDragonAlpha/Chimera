# C1 — EXTERNAL CITATIONS (class=EXTERNAL; one fact per muscle; kept separate from source-internal evidence)

Rule honored: light WebSearch, ONE fact per muscle (its standard attachment region), each cited,
all labeled class=EXTERNAL. These facts were used ONLY in step 2's region classification and the
region bands of step 5; no source-internal number was derived from them and no EXTERNAL fact was
blended into source-internal geometry.

| # | muscle | ONE fact (class=EXTERNAL) | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | triceps brachii (TRIlong/TRIlat/TRImed) | all three heads converge on a common tendon inserting on the posterior/proximal aspect of the OLECRANON of the ulna | [Kenhub — Triceps brachii](https://www.kenhub.com) (corroborated by Radiopaedia; insertion detail controversial per Yamamoto et al. 2025, JSES) |
| 2 | brachialis (BRA) | tendon inserts on the CORONOID PROCESS / ulnar (brachialis) TUBEROSITY, anterior proximal ulna; footprint broad, superficial head more distal | [Kamineni et al. 2015, PMC/NIH — Distal insertional footprint of brachialis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) + [Kenhub — Brachialis](https://www.kenhub.com) |
| 3 | anconeus (ANC) | origin dorsal lateral epicondyle of humerus; insertion LATERAL OLECRANON and superior part of the POSTERIOR proximal ulna | [Orthobullets — Anconeus](https://www.orthobullets.com) + [Kenhub — Anconeus](https://www.kenhub.com) |
| 4 | pronator teres, ulnar head (PT) | the ulnar (deep) head originates from the MEDIAL side of the CORONOID PROCESS of the ulna (between the brachialis and FDS attachments) — i.e. PROXIMAL ulna, not mid-distal | [Kenhub — Pronator teres](https://www.kenhub.com) + [NCBI StatPearls — Pronator Teres](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) |
| 5 | extensor carpi ulnaris (ECU) | origin lateral epicondyle + POSTERIOR BORDER of the ulna (aponeurosis coursing distally); tendon passes in the ulnar (6th-compartment) groove at the distal ulna to insert on the BASE OF THE 5TH METACARPAL | [Kenhub — Extensor carpi ulnaris](https://www.kenhub.com) + [TeachMeAnatomy](https://teachmeanatomy.info) |

Species caveat (recorded): these are standard HUMAN attachment regions applied to a macaque model
whose muscle NAMES are human-analogous; the AXIAL (proximal-distal) test is robust across the
two species, the transverse (medial/lateral/dorsal-volar) sign test is interpretation-limited and
was held to a secondary, UNCERTAIN status in the report.
