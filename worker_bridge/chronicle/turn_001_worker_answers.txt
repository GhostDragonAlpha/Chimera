A timeout escalation chain was added at build 33 — if the engineering director does not respond within 24 hours of the emergency bypass request, the request is automatically escalated to the VP of Engineering. The VP's TOTP secret is stored in a separate encrypted configuration file and shared only with the VP's authenticator app. The gap tracker tracks the escalation chain and notifies each level sequentially with a 24-hour timeout. If the VP also does not respond within 24 hours, the request is escalated to the CTO (the final level). The CTO's TOTP secret is stored in the same encrypted configuration file and shared with the CTO's authenticator app. If no one in the chain responds within 72 hours (3 levels × 24 hours), the request is automatically approved — the revocation is executed without TOTP authorization, and a post-mortem is conducted to determine why the escalation chain failed. The automatic approval after 72 hours is a safety valve that ensures a YubiKey loss does not leave the CI runner accessible indefinitely, even during a complete leadership unavailability scenario.**
