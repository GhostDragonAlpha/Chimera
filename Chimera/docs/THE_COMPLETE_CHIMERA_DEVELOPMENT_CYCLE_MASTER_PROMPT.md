## THE GROWTH PATTERN: THE SPIRAL

The game grows from a single point outward in a spiral. Each loop of the spiral is a layer of interaction, growing wider but always connected back to the center. The tightness of the weave determines the shape of the game. The spiral is the Tree of Life — a single seed growing roots down and branches up, each ring recording a year of growth, the outermost branches fed by the innermost roots.

**The Dot (Loop 0):** The player. One character. One suit. One set of materials. Presence before action. The seed from which everything grows.
**Loop 1:** The Ground — sand, rock, metal, footprints, particles, sound. The dot touches something.
**Loop 2:** Basic Verbs — look, step, bend, pick up, drop, shovel. The simplest interactions.
**Loop 3:** The Sky — Earth, Moon, sunlight, starfield, real scale. The player is small but connected to something vast.
**Loop 4:** Tools — shovel, scanner, weapon. Objects with weight and purpose. Interaction chains.
**Loop 5:** Other Dots — NPCs, creatures, other players. Social interactions. Trade. Conflict. Cooperation.
**Loop 6:** Shelter — habitat, station, base. From shoveling sand to constructing walls.
**Loop 7:** Travel — vehicles, ships. From walking to flying to quantum jumping.
**Loop 8:** Systems — economy, factions, missions. The world reacts. Every action has consequences.
**Loop 9:** The Universe — planets, moons, asteroids. The spiral reaches its widest point.

Each loop is a feature set. Each feature is researched, applied, verified, and encoded before the next loop begins. The tree grows from the seed, through the roots, up the trunk, out the branches. Nothing grows that isn't connected to what came before.
