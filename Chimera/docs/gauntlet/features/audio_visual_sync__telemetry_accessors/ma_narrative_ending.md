# Master: the sacrifice economy — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

Connection to the Costless Life failure ending, stated by refusal: this feature
never costs the player anything and never writes to the sacrifice log — walking
must stay free, because the sacrifice economy only means something against a
baseline of unpriced, bodily, trustworthy motion. A game that charged you for
believable footsteps would cheapen every real cost above it.

Where it does touch the ending's machinery: the dim-star diagnostic reads a
LIFE of choices, and choices are made by a player who stayed — players stay in
worlds whose ground answers them. So the feature's contribution to the
Costless Life arc is indirect but real: it protects the embodied baseline that
makes cost feel costly. It knows where it stands — outside the ledger, holding
the floor the ledger sits on.
