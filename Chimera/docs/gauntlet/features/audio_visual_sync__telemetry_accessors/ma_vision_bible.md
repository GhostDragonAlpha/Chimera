# Master: vision fit — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

The STORY_BIBLE phrase this serves is "Those who love" — read through the
studio's own gloss: love here is attention that keeps its promises. A world
that answers your boots on time, every time, is the smallest continuous act of
that attention; the accessors are how the promise is kept when no one is
watching.

Honest self-score: vision_fit 1.0 — system infrastructure; it supports the
vision without directly expressing it (the visionkeeper gave
Demo_Phase2_DemoTerminal the same judgment class, and this feature deserves no
inflation). Defense of the score in three sentences: it makes no image, tells
no story, and costs no sacrifice, so it cannot score above neutral; but every
resonant-minimalism scene stands on ground that must never audibly lie, so it
cannot score below neutral either; 1.0 is what faithful plumbing earns under
a Design Law that reserves multipliers for features players can feel as
meaning.
