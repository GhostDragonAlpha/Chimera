# Middle: readability — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

This feature's readability is AURAL, but it still has a silhouette: the crunch
must be separable from wind at walk height, which is a contrast problem in the
mix rather than the palette. Against regolith-grey visuals, the footstep is
often the strongest proof of ground the player gets — the tri-pads pain taught
us surfaces can read uniformly dark in situ, and when the eye loses the ground
the EAR must hold it. Readability rules: footsteps sit above the wind bed in
the mix at walking distance zero; per-surface variation (sand vs rock vs metal)
is the audible palette; and the telemetry accessors are how we prove the
contrast survived a mix change — a latency histogram that suddenly widens after
an audio commit is a readability regression the eye would never catch.
