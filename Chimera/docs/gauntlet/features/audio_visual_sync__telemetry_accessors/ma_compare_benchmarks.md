# Master: against the canon — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

- **No Man's Sky** — its per-biome footstep mapping is the scale ambition, but
  its sync visibly slops under frame drops (audio rides notifies uncompensated).
  Where Chimera stands: smaller world, harder promise — we steal the
  per-surface material mapping idea; we refuse the unmeasured trust in
  notifies, which is the entire reason the accessors exist. Verdict: behind on
  variety, ahead (by design) on verified timing.
- **Star Citizen** — the maximalist body-audio simulation: breath, suit,
  servos, footfalls all coupled; gorgeous and famously fragile under its own
  complexity. Where Chimera stands: we steal the lesson that body audio IS
  presence; we refuse the coupling — one measured channel (footfall sync)
  guarded by telemetry beats a symphony nobody can regression-test. Verdict:
  we will never match its density, and audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors
  is the bet that a small promise KEPT reads more AAA than a grand one that
  stutters.
