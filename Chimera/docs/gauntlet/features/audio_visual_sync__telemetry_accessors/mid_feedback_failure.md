# Middle: how failure feels — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

Player-side failure here is DESYNC, and its diegetic "no" must never exist —
this feature's failure states are for the studio, not the player. When the sync
budget fails, the world's honest tell is the wrongness itself: crunches
arriving like an echo of your feet instead of your feet. No tooltip could help;
the fix is upstream.

Studio-side, the diegetic no IS the telemetry: count frozen at 0 while the
pawn walks is the system showing us numbness without a single log line of
prose; latency pinned at its 999 ms initializer is the instrument reporting its
own disconnection. audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors turns silent failure
into visible failure — which is the only kind that gets fixed.
