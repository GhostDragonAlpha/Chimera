# Middle: the feedback triad — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

Visual channel: the boot contact and the dust kick (Ground_Sand_Footprints'
territory) — lose it and the sound floats free of the body, becoming radio
static. Audio channel: the crunch itself — lose it and the moon goes dead
underfoot; the whole feature exists to keep this channel honest in time. The
kinesthetic channel: the subtle camera bob that peaks with each contact —
lose it and sound+sight still agree but the BODY stops believing; motion is
what makes the ear's evidence feel like weight.

The feature's job is the agreement of all three within one perceptual moment;
its telemetry is the referee that says which channel drifted when they stop
agreeing — for audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors, a triad that cannot be
measured is a triad that will quietly fall apart.
