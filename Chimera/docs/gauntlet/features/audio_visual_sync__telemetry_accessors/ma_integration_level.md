# Master: placement in the Yard — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

Where it lives in L_RegolithYard: everywhere boots touch, but its PROVING
ground is the tri-pad approach — the one route that crosses metal (habitat
ramp), packed sand (the walk path), and rock (the pad aprons) inside thirty
seconds of walking. That route is the level's built-in test fixture.

The landmark that makes it findable (to testers and beats, not players): the
habitat ramp lip — the metal-to-sand threshold is a single visible seam with a
guaranteed material change on the very next footfall, so any human or
sleepwalker can audit the sync and the surface swap in one stride. Sightline:
from the ramp you can SEE the full walk path before walking it, which teaches
the route the beat will take. What placement says about intended frequency:
constant — this feature is load-bearing on every meter of the level, which is
exactly why its evidence route was chosen where three surfaces meet closest.
