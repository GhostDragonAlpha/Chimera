# Master: a wordless moment — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

The micro-moment it can host, with zero text: the player walks off the metal
habitat ramp onto sand for the first time — clank, clank, then hush-crunch —
and in that single synced transition the world says "you have left the built
thing; the moon holds you now." No dialogue, no UI. The whole meaning lives in
the material change arriving exactly on the footfall; 100 ms of slop and it
becomes a sound bug instead of a threshold crossed.

What the player understands afterward that they didn't before: surfaces here
are TRUE — the ground is not scenery but substance. That trust, planted
wordlessly in one step, is what every later mechanic (digging, footprints,
trade in far-off stations) quietly spends. audio_visual_sync/
telemetry_accessors is the unseen witness that keeps the threshold moment
honest in every build.
