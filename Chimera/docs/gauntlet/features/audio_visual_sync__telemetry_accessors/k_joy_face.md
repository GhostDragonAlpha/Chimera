# Kindergarten: the face — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

The face is the small unconscious SMILE of rightness — the one players make
when a world feels solid without knowing why. It happens the first time a boot
lands and the regolith crunch arrives in the same instant as the footfall and
the dust kick. Nothing on screen points at it; the face is caused by absence of
wrongness. The inverse face — the squint of something's-off — is what
audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors exists to catch before a player ever
wears it: it timestamps the footfall and the sound so the studio sees the
squint as a number (latency ms) instead of a lost player.
