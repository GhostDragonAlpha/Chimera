# Master: the emotion arc — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

Hour one (discovery): the designed emotion is GROUNDEDNESS — the low hum of
"this place is real" that synced boots buy before the player has done anything.
It is not excitement; it is the absence of the uncanny, which is what lets
every other emotion land later.

Hour ten (fluency): footsteps recede into the body schema — the player stops
hearing them the way you stop hearing your own heartbeat. That recession is
SUCCESS, not boredom: the feature is designed to disappear. Boredom risk lives
elsewhere — a soundscape too static across ten hours — and the answer is not
juicing footsteps but widening the world's audible palette (weather, machines,
distant activity) over that same arc.

The honest asymmetry: this feature can only ever LOSE emotion (one desync
snaps groundedness and hands the player thirty minutes of noticing). Designing
for it means designing its measurement — which is the feature — so the emotion
it guards is the trust it never lets break. audio_visual_sync/
telemetry_accessors earns hour ten by being unheard in hour one.
