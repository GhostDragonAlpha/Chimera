# Kindergarten: the spark — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

The fun, for a child: your boots make the moon talk — every step answers you
from the ground, exactly when your foot lands.

The toy test, honestly run: give a player 60 seconds with no goal, no reward,
no instructions, and synced footsteps ARE a toy — people immediately try
rhythms: walk, sprint, stop dead, shuffle, stomp in place to hear the crunch
answer their feet. The sync is the whole toy; a late crunch kills it. But the
telemetry ACCESSORS themselves are not the toy — they are the toy's nervous
system, invisible to the player. What earns the fun is the sound-on-time; what
audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors buys is our ability to PROVE the
sound-on-time, every night, without a human ear. The missing piece today is
that the nervous system reports numbness (count=0) even while feet are moving.
