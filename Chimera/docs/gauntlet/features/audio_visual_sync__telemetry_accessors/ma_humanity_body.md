# Master: the player's body — audio_visual_sync/telemetry_accessors

Motion sickness: footstep-synced camera bob is a known comfort lever — bob
amplitude must stay small and FREQUENCY-locked to real footfalls; desynced bob
(visual rhythm disagreeing with audible rhythm) is a nausea multiplier, which
makes the sync window a comfort feature, not just a fidelity one. FOV is
untouched by this feature; keep it that way.

Photosensitivity/flash: no flashing surface — the feature emits sound and
timestamps, nothing strobes.

Fatigue: held-W locomotion is already low-strain; the feature adds no
hold-to-repeat demands and no reach — its riskiest bodily interaction is
listening fatigue from repetitive identical crunches over long sessions, and
the ±4% pitch variance is the mitigation (variation without timing cost).
Comfort summary: the accessors let us PROVE the audio-visual-vestibular
agreement that comfort research keeps pointing at, on every nightly build.
