Use of Cookies

This Site uses “cookies”. Cookies are text files containing small amounts of information which are downloaded to your device when you visit a website. Cookies are then sent back to the originating website on each subsequent visit, or to another website that recognizes that cookie. Cookies are widely used because they allow a website to recognize a user’s device and therefore allow enhanced functionality.

The IAEA uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • to compile statistical data on the use of this Site and its functioning, so that we can monitor and improve it;
