How the mechanism worksHEAT → GAS → MOTION

A sealed gas does work on a loaded beam.

Heating nitrogen raises its pressure. The bellows expands against gravity and an elastic beam; damping turns motion into heat. Release the heater to let the mechanism settle.

Ideal sealed bellows and ideal elastic beam. No leakage, plasticity, or heat-loss model. Nitrogen starts at 320 K. The beam uses the NIST 6061 aluminum curve at 290 K; gravity derives from JPL GM. The model library is tested against Cantera.
