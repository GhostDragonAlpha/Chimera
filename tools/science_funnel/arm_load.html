Model, energy account & sourcesWHAT THIS ARM REPRESENTS

One elbow bears a modeled load

The shoulder is fixed. The elbow angle and angular speed are native dynamic states; the browser never poses the bones. A limited actuator works toward the requested angle using a finite ideal mechanical store.

  • The macaque geometry is source-derived. Segment mass and inertia describe the modeled moving assembly, not the isolated bone mesh.
  • The drive, hand contact proxy, support and applied downward load are authored mechanisms. This is not a biological muscle, grip model or full animal.
  • The support is fixed for a trial. Reset explicitly prepares the joint at 90° with zero speed and refills the store to 1 J.
  • Earth gravity acts on the modeled assembly. Atmospheric values below are reference conditions only: arm air drag and heat exchange are not implemented.
  • Diagnostic colors and lighting distinguish the geometry; they do not represent measured optical properties.
