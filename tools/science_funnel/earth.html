A local experiment in Earth conditions

The native engine advances the sample. This page displays its metric triangles and captured readings; orbiting the camera does not move the physical scene.

  • The macaque upper limb is a jointed anatomical reference. Its pose and held attachment are kinematic. Muscle forces, grip dynamics and a whole animal are outside this experiment.
  • Earth gravity is constant within this bounded patch, derived from pinned JPL Earth GM and a WGS84 origin. A NASA low-altitude atmosphere fit is sampled once per trial and treated as uniform here. This is not weather or scanned terrain.
  • The reference body translates without rolling: mass 0.5 g, support radius 15 mm, drag coefficient 0.47, heat capacity 0.5 J/K, heat transfer coefficient 8 W/m²K, initial temperature 295 K. These are authored reference coefficients, not measured macaque tissue or a measured natural object.
  • Turning air off creates an evacuated comparison with Earth gravity retained. Applying environment settings resets position and velocity at the current hand attachment.
  • Colors and lighting distinguish the components; they do not represent measured optical properties. The finite ground patch and simple contact model do not describe a full planet.
