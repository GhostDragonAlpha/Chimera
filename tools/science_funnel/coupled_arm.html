Coupling, energy account & sourcesWHAT THIS ARM REPRESENTS

Two coordinates, shared motion

The mount is fixed while shoulder flexion and elbow flexion evolve together. The native solver uses the coupled mass matrix and velocity-dependent loads; the browser displays the returned geometry without posing or animating the bones.

  • Switch off one drive to see how the other joint can still move it. A disabled drive does not lock a joint.
  • Cut all power to remove motor torque. Gravity, the hand load, damping and joint stops still act.
  • The source-derived macaque anatomy is a modeled assembly. The drives and hand load are authored mechanisms, not biological muscles.
  • Joint limits are shoulder −75° to 90° and elbow 20° to 140°. Stop reactions act at these limits; there is no hand–surface contact.
  • Reset restores the pose, speed, physical clock and 2 J store, retains your other settings, and resumes.
  • Diagnostic colors and lighting distinguish geometry; they are not measured optical properties.
